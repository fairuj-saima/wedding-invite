Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

Traditional or semi-formal attire in festive colors is perfect for the evening. We kindly ask guests to avoid wearing gold colored sarees and prince coats to respect the bride and groom's wishes.

Will parking be available?

Yes, parking will be available at The Waterfront Convention Hall.

What time should I arrive?

We kindly recommend arriving around 7:00 PM to allow plenty of time to settle in, join us for our Nikah ceremony, and enjoy the evening.
